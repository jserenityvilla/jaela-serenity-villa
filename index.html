Please follow these simple instructions when using the appliances.

  • Press the Power button on the remote.
  • Select HDMI or Smart TV.
  • Netflix and YouTube are available.
  • Turn the TV off before leaving.

❄ Air Conditioner

  • Recommended temperature: 24°C.
  • Keep doors and windows closed.
  • Turn off when leaving the room.

  • Use the gas cooktop for daily cooking.
